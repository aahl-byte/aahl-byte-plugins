# Research catalogue

The open questions for <project>. Each entry is an independently answerable question with competing
hypotheses and a falsifiable criterion, in the schema from the research plugin's `METHOD.md`. A
hypothesis wins for a domain, not globally. The index is the work queue, sorted by kill-risk; the area
sections hold the full entries. When a result is folded, its answer goes back into its entry.

## Index

Kill-risk is how much of the project dies if the answer is bad, not how likely a bad answer is.

Status is `open`, `spiking`, `parked` (by a dated direction ruling), or the result's verdict, copied exactly. A question with only pre-programme results stays `open`, and its entry links them on a **Prior evidence:** line.
